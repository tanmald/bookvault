## Why BookVault?

Your books deserve better than scattered folders and manual spreadsheets.

**The Problem:** Digital book collections are messy. You download EPUBs and PDFs, they end up in random folders, and you can't remember what you've read or where you saved it.

**The Solution:** BookVault uses AI to automatically organize your library. Upload your books, and we handle the rest—extracting titles, authors, genres, and cover images. Share collections with friends, track your reading progress, and access everything from any device.

**What makes it different:**
- 🤖 **AI-powered metadata extraction** — No manual data entry
- 👥 **Social library sharing** — Invite friends to see your collection
- 📱 **Works everywhere** — Phone, tablet, or computer
- 🔒 **Privacy-first** — You control who sees your books

## ✨ Features

### 🤖 AI-Powered Organization
Watch your library organize itself automatically:
- **Smart Extraction** — AI extracts titles, authors, genres, and cover images from your EPUB/PDF files
- **Instant Organization** — Upload via drag-and-drop and see your library come to life
- **Multiple Collections** — Create separate libraries for different purposes (personal, work, book clubs)

### 📖 Track Your Reading Journey
Never forget a book again:
- **Reading Status** — Mark books as "Want to Read," "Reading," or "Completed"
- **Visual Progress** — Track how far you've read with progress indicators
- **Reading History** — See everything you've read at a glance

### 👥 Share & Connect
Your reading journey, shared:
- **Private Sharing** — Invite friends with secure, private links
- **Discover Together** — See what friends are reading and get inspired
- **Book Clubs** — Perfect for sharing reading lists and tracking group progress

### 🎨 Beautifully Designed
Built for readers, by readers:
- **Flexible Views** — Browse in grid, list, or kanban layouts
- **Light & Dark Themes** — Choose what's comfortable for your eyes
- **Multi-Language** — Available in English and Portuguese
- **Responsive Design** — Looks great on any device

## 🚀 Getting Started

Get your library organized in 30 seconds:

### 1. Create Your Account
Sign up in seconds with just your email.

### 2. Upload Your Books
Drag and drop your EPUB or PDF files. BookVault's AI will extract all the details automatically.

### 3. Share & Enjoy
Invite friends to see your collection and start tracking your reading progress across all your devices.
