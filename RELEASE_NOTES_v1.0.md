## Changelog Version

### Share Your Reading Journey with Friends

BookVault is here! Your digital library is now beautifully organized and ready to share. Upload your books once, and let BookVault handle the rest—from organizing your library to tracking your reading journey.

**What's New:**
- **Effortless Book Organization** — Upload EPUB and PDF files and watch your library organize itself automatically
- **AI-Powered Metadata Extraction** — Book details are automatically extracted using AI—no more manual data entry for titles, authors, genres, or cover images
- **Smart Collections** — Create multiple libraries for different purposes (personal reading, work, book clubs, etc.)
- **Flexible Views** — Browse your books in grid, list, or kanban views—whatever works best for you
- **Reading Progress Tracking** — Mark books as "Want to Read," "Reading," or "Completed" with visual progress indicators
- **Social Library Sharing** — Invite friends to see your collection with secure, private links and discover what they're reading
- **Privacy-First Design** — You control who sees your books—everything is private by default
- **Multi-Language Support** — Available in English and Portuguese
- **Light & Dark Themes** — Choose the theme that's comfortable for your eyes
- **Responsive Design** — Access your library from any device—phone, tablet, or computer

**How to Get Started:**
1. Create your account in seconds with just your email
2. Upload your books via drag-and-drop
3. Watch AI extract book details automatically
4. Invite friends and start sharing your reading journey

**Availability:** Available to all new users immediately

## What's Included in v1.0

**Core Features:**
- User authentication and account management
- Library management with multiple collections
- Book upload (EPUB and PDF support)
- AI-powered metadata extraction (OpenAI integration)
- Reading progress tracking (Want to Read / Reading / Completed)
- Friend connections and library sharing
- Invite system with secure codes
- Responsive grid and kanban book views
- Advanced filtering and search
- Dark/light theme support
- Internationalization (English/Portuguese)

**Technical Stack:**
- React 18 + TypeScript + Vite
- Supabase backend (auth, database, storage)
- TanStack Query for data fetching
- shadcn/ui component library
- Tailwind CSS for styling
- Real-time sync across devices
